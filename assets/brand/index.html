05 — Usage

Keep it consistent

Do — give the mark clear space equal to half its width.
Don't — recolor the mark outside the palette or add shadows/gradients of your own.
Do — use the reversed mark on clay or dark backgrounds.
Don't — stretch, rotate, or outline the wordmark.
Do — pair one clay accent with plenty of paper.
Don't — set the wordmark in any font other than Fraunces.
